# Gemini SEO Skills

This folder contains 13 Gemini-oriented SEO skills and an optional SEO Director agent. Skill directories retain the underscore naming used by the Gemini package.

## Included skills

- `seo_technical_auditor`
- `seo_manager_agent`
- `seo_schema_generator`
- `seo_eeat_builder`
- `seo_competitor_analyzer`
- `seo_director_agent`
- `seo_content_pruner`
- `seo_on_page_optimizer`
- `seo_content_writer`
- `seo_proposal_builder`
- `seo_local_strategist`
- `seo_internal_linker`
- `seo_off_page_pr`

## Privacy and scope

The proprietary keyword scraper and its standalone skill instructions are intentionally excluded. The manager, director, and content-writer workflows use user-provided keyword data or explicitly approved research sources. They do not require the private scraper.

Publishing, live-site changes, outreach, paid placements, software installation, and recurring monitoring require explicit user authorization. The skills provide workflows and drafts; they do not guarantee rankings.
